Reflow — Privacy Policy

Effective date: March 24, 2026

Reflow is a browser extension that automatically refreshes web pages at user-configured intervals. This policy explains how Reflow handles your data.

Data Collection

Reflow does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. The extension operates entirely within your browser.

Data Storage

Reflow stores the following data locally on your device using Chrome's built-in storage API (chrome.storage.local):

— Your refresh interval preferences per website domain
— Active refresh timer states (which tabs are being refreshed)
— Option settings (hard refresh, scroll preservation, randomization, notifications)

This data never leaves your browser. It is not sent to any server, third-party service, or analytics platform.

Scroll Position

When "Keep scroll" is enabled, Reflow temporarily saves your scroll position using the browser's sessionStorage before each refresh and restores it after. This data is automatically cleared when you close the tab.

Website Content

Reflow does not read, analyze, collect, or transmit any content from the web pages you visit. The extension only reloads pages and displays a countdown indicator.

Permissions

Reflow requests browser permissions solely to provide its core functionality: scheduling refresh timers (alarms), saving your preferences (storage), reloading pages (scripting, tabs), and showing the in-page countdown (host permissions). No permission is used for data collection purposes.

Third-Party Services

Reflow does not integrate with any third-party services, analytics tools, advertising networks, or external servers.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new effective date.

Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, contact the developer through the Chrome Web Store support link on the Reflow extension listing page.
